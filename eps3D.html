What It Is

Structure, insulation and form —
in a single panel.

ECO-GEO EPS 3D Panels combine a lightweight EPS core with a structural wire space-frame, finished with shotcrete or concrete on site. The result is a monolithic wall, slab or foundation element that carries load, insulates, and installs fast — with dramatically less material and labour than traditional systems.

Panel Anatomy

What's inside an ECO-GEO panel.

Four engineered components combine to form a structural, insulated, load-bearing system — each performing a distinct structural role.

01
EPS Core
Expanded polystyrene body provides lightweight insulation, dimensional stability, and the structural cavity fill.
02
Wire Space Frame
Galvanized steel truss welded through the core transfers tension and compression loads across the entire panel.
03
Shotcrete / Concrete
Sprayed or cast concrete shells on both faces convert the panel into a monolithic structural composite.
04
Connections
Panel-to-panel and panel-to-foundation ties create continuous load paths across the assembled structure.

Build Sequence

From panel to structure, in four steps.

A single coordinated sequence replaces the traditional layers of formwork, masonry, insulation and rendering.

01

Panel Positioning

Pre-fabricated ECO-GEO panels are craned or manually positioned and aligned to setting-out lines. No on-site cutting, no formwork.

02

Connection & Bracing

Adjacent panels are tied together using the embedded connectors, and temporary bracing holds alignment until the concrete shells set.

03

Concrete Application

Shotcrete or poured concrete is applied to both faces of the panel. The wire space-frame becomes the reinforcing steel of the composite wall.

04

Finish & Cure

Once cured, the panel is a monolithic structural element ready for rendering, cladding, or direct finishes — with insulation already built in.
