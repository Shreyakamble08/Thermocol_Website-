Product Range

Two product families.

RGBS mouldings and panels — precision-crafted EPS profiles finished with the strongest mineral coatings for exterior durability.

Family 01

RGBS Mouldings

For windows, doors, floor-to-floor separation.

RGBS mouldings offer enhanced architectural beauty for the building facade. The major advantage is the capability to customize for large quantity requirements. Standard designs are available for retail projects.

Family 02

RGBS Panels

Wider design variety with tongue-and-groove joints.

RGBS panels are created with multiple possibilities, offering wider varieties of design to architects and end users. The joints between panels are designed using a precise tongue-and- groove method for seamless installation.
