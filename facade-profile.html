Product Range

Two product families.

RGBS mouldings and panels — precision-crafted EPS profiles with mineral coatings.

Family 01

RGBS Mouldings.

RGBS mouldings offer mouldings for windows, doors and floor-to-floor separation to enhance the architectural beauty of the building facade. The major advantage is the capability to customise for large quantity requirements. Standard designs are available for retail projects.

  • Window surrounds, door frames, floor separation
  • Customisable for large-quantity projects
  • Standard designs for retail

RGBS Panels.

RGBS panels are created with multiple possibilities offering wider varieties of design to architects and end users. The joints between the panels are designed with a tongue-and-groove method for precise alignment and seamless installation.
