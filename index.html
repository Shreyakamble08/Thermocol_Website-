Who We Are

A leading manufacturer of Expanded Polystyrene in Maharashtra.

Operating since 2016, Rishabh Thermocole Arts has been catering to the EPS packaging and insulation needs of clients across multiple sectors. Our manufacturing facility at MIDC Talegaon, Dabhade operates with an installed capacity of 360 tonnes per annum.

01 Vision

A multi-product insulation & packaging conglomerate.

To build a multi-product insulation and packaging conglomerate with a relentless focus on quality and sustainability delivering dependable EPS solutions that support modern construction, industry and everyday life.

02 Philosophy

Highest quality work that meets your expectations.

Quality comes with integrity — using the right density, the right preparation and cooling times, and the right processes. Our in-house team pays detailed attention at every stage, and our vendor relationships form a critical part of our value chain.

03 Infrastructure

Engineered at our MIDC Talegaon facility.

Our existing plant at Talegaon, Pune spans a total area of 10,500 sq ft and is equipped with a complete EPS production line — from raw bead handling to precision CNC cutting and hand fabrication.
