100× lighter than soil. Proven for 30+ years.

Geofoam is a lightweight, rigid foam plastic used worldwide as a fill material for more than 30 years. At approximately 100 times lighter than soil and 20–30× lighter than other lightweight fill alternatives, it dramatically reduces loads on underlying soils and enables faster construction without staged work.

Why Geofoam

Engineered for civil infrastructure.

EPS geofoam can be covered to look like a normal sloped embankment, finished as a wall, or used as fill to reduce loads on underlying soils — enabling highways and infrastructure to be built quickly without staged construction.
