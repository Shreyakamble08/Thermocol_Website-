02 Fix Board Fixing Adhesive Structural adhesive fixing the board to the substrate.

04 Bond Base Coat Cementitious layer binding mesh to insulation.

Performance

Engineered performance, independently validated.

01

Durability

Formulated with 100% acrylic binder — resists fading, chalking and yellowing. Color is integral to the finish, so scratches reveal the same color beneath. Highly crack-resistant due to system flexibility. Passed the Miami-Dade Hurricane Test.

02

Design Flexibility

Virtually limitless colors and textures. Can be fashioned into almost any shape — cornices, arches, columns, keystones, cornerstones, special moldings, and decorative accents that would be cost-prohibitive using conventional construction.

03

Moisture Control

Oak Ridge National Laboratory research — supported by the US Department of Energy — validated EIFS as the best-performing cladding for thermal and moisture control compared to brick, stucco and fiber cement. Requires correct installation and proper detailing.
